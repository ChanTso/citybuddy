## Goal

Add the commerce-owned sensitive-action boundary that prepares an owner- and turn-bound
PendingAction, then confirms it by atomically validating and consuming that action, executing one
legal refund through the verified CB-116/CB-117 boundaries, and persisting one immutable
ActionReceipt. Retries converge on durable MySQL truth and cannot execute the business mutation
twice.

## In scope

- Add closed-schema internal prepare and confirm endpoints under the existing exact-scope Agent OBO
  boundary. Identity, owner, actor, support session, trace, turn, scope, and evaluation sandbox come
  from validated credentials and server context, not model-controlled fields.
- Introduce commerce-owned PendingAction and ActionReceipt migrations, repositories, exact runtime
  grants, and OpenAPI. PendingAction commits action type, canonical arguments, owner/session/turn,
  target identity/version, expiry, sandbox, state/version, and server idempotency. ActionReceipt
  commits the immutable authoritative result and the same identity/intent boundary.
- Keep a closed server-owned action registry whose initial action is the existing owner-scoped refund
  request. Callers cannot select implementation, URL, SQL, scope, owner, resource version, or receipt
  shape.
- On prepare, validate exact schema, authorization, ownership, refund eligibility, current version,
  and canonical intent, then persist or replay one PendingAction.
- On confirm, lock and re-read PendingAction and required refund/payment truth. In one
  `commerce_db` transaction validate every binding and expiry, execute the legal refund through the
  verified CB-117 boundary, consume PendingAction once, and persist immutable ActionReceipt.
- Treat ActionReceipt as the sole confirmation-success truth. Same-intent replay returns it without
  another refund, ledger movement, Outbox row, consume, or receipt.
- Consume CB-116 committed-payment closure and CB-117 bounded refund recovery as verified
  dependencies. Do not redesign either boundary or reintroduce transaction-external locking
  validation.
- Return only the bounded receipt view needed by CB-121. Agent projection, public SSE, confirmation
  prose, and model invocation remain outside commerce.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Prepare and confirm require the exact Agent OBO issuer, purpose/type, commerce audience, agent
   actor, action scope, user subject, verified support session, time bounds, and exact evaluation
   sandbox equality/liveness where applicable. Wrong modes, scope, session, owner, or sandbox fail
   closed.
2. The action registry and request schemas are closed and bounded. Unknown action/fields,
   non-canonical arguments, caller-selected identity/version/implementation, and unsupported
   mutation reject before persistence.
3. One canonical prepare intent creates one bounded-expiry PendingAction. Same intent replays it;
   any changed action, arguments, owner, session, turn, target, version, or sandbox conflicts without
   another row.
4. Confirm revalidates authorization, intent, owner, session, turn, target version, expiry, sandbox,
   and refund eligibility inside the transaction. Missing, duplicate, malformed, consumed, stale,
   or contradictory truth cannot mutate business state.
5. Successful confirm validates and consumes PendingAction, performs one legal refund mutation,
   writes required ledger/Outbox truth, and persists one immutable ActionReceipt in a single MySQL
   transaction. Controlled failure at every write boundary rolls the whole unit back.
6. ActionReceipt has stable identity and complete immutable canonical commitment to the action and
   authoritative result. Each content-bearing column is anchored or has a documented enforced
   invariant.
7. Concurrent identical confirms and response-loss replay converge to one consumed action, one
   refund result, one allowed ledger/Outbox effect, and one receipt. Every successful caller obtains
   the same durable result; changed intent conflicts.
8. Recognized contention uses CB-117's bounded post-rollback recovery and immutable validated truth.
   Observation failure remains typed indeterminate; it is never absence, conflict, denial, success,
   dependency unavailability, or an unclassified 500.
9. Receipt replay reconciles the complete PendingAction, ActionReceipt, refund, payment, ledger, and
   Outbox closure. Missing, duplicate, contradictory, or partially persisted truth fails closed and
   never triggers a second mutation.
10. MySQL remains the sole action truth. Model text, request bodies, agent projection, Redis, message
    delivery, and in-memory state cannot authorize confirm or establish success.
11. Exact migrations/grants permit only required `commerce_app` DML. `agent_app` reaches the action
    boundary only through authenticated HTTP and cannot access commerce truth; other runtime
    identities cannot mutate the new family.
12. No agent-side confirmation, receipt projection, turn commit, public receipt event, generalized
    recovery scanner, global lock protocol, refund state redesign, new action, or later behavior is
    introduced.

## Rejection paths

- Reject wrong token mode/issuer/audience/actor/scope, forged/cross-user session, body identity,
  sandbox mismatch/inactivity/indeterminacy, and authorization rendered as success or absence.
- Reject unknown or oversized action input, caller-selected implementation/owner/version/scope,
  incomplete canonical intent comparison, and changed-intent replay.
- Reject confirmation outside one transaction, consume before a failed mutation, mutation without
  receipt, receipt without mutation, split refund/ledger/Outbox writes, or any locking validation
  performed after the bounded transaction returns.
- Reject expired, consumed, stale, wrong-owner/session/turn/sandbox, altered-argument, ineligible,
  over-refund, unpaid/cancelled, missing, duplicate, or contradictory durable truth.
- Reject concurrency that leaks raw lock failures, treats failed observation as a business result,
  performs a second final mutation, or bypasses CB-117 recovery.
- Reject mutable/caller-authored receipt truth, direct database access by `agent_app`, broad grants,
  generalized action engines, agent projection/SSE, or CB-121 and later work.

## Out of scope

- redesign of CB-116 payment truth/visibility or CB-117 refund contention recovery;
- global PaymentLockContext or acyclic lock protocol, refund state-machine redesign, new sensitive
  actions, provider settlement, and chargeback;
- agent confirmation-text interpretation, agent PendingAction reference, receipt projection, turn
  commit, SSE event, and post-commit explanation retry owned by CB-121;
- handoff, memory, failure export, web, observability, deployment, real-provider access, and measured
  performance;
- background expiry deletion or generalized action recovery scanning.

## Required evidence

- executable exact-schema and complete canonical-commitment tests for PendingAction/ActionReceipt,
  including every content-bearing column, same-intent replay, changed intent, and immutable result;
- real OBO owner/session/turn/sandbox authorization and rejection evidence with no body-identity or
  model-consent shortcut;
- real MySQL prepare uniqueness, expiry, current target/version anchoring, and one atomic
  consume/refund/ledger/Outbox/receipt transaction with controlled rollback at every write point;
- deterministic concurrent confirm, response loss, restart, and replay proving one mutation and one
  receipt, plus typed CB-117 contention and true-unavailable evidence;
- closure fault injection across PendingAction, ActionReceipt, refund, payment/order, ledger, and
  Outbox for missing, duplicate, and contradictory truth without a second mutation;
- exact migration/runtime grant and cross-database denial evidence;
- OpenAPI and contract tests for prepare/confirm success and all bounded rejection families;
- regressions proving CB-116/CB-117 contracts and the direct-user refund lifecycle remain unchanged;
- evidence that CB-121 projection/turn/SSE and all later work were not implemented early;
- complete-diff self-review, fresh independent read-only review, final-head CI, the unique closeout
  `make ci`, and factual completion evidence.
