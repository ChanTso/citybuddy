## Goal

Add the smallest operational metrics and an optional no-op-capable trace mirror needed to observe the
verified portfolio paths after CB-140, without creating another business/evidence truth source or
expanding into unimplemented Agent confirmation, memory, handoff, or export behavior.

## In scope

- Define a bounded, low-cardinality metric inventory for verified identity, catalog, standard
  ordering, seckill reservation/order, mock payment/refund, RAG, chat, and PendingAction
  prepare/clarification/decline/expiry paths.
- Record request count, outcome class, duration, and the smallest path-specific counters needed by
  CB-152, including FAQ cache lookup/hit/miss and model-call avoided/issued counters with exact
  definitions. Do not use user, token, session, turn, order, reservation, prompt, or free-text values
  as labels.
- Keep metrics observational. Existing MySQL/Redis/Elasticsearch/`cs_db` owners remain authoritative,
  and metric loss, duplication, or backend outage cannot change a public response or state transition.
- Provide an optional trace adapter for already implemented request/agent spans. Disabled or
  unavailable tracing degrades to an explicit no-op; trace export is never required for business
  success, evaluation evidence, or CB-152 assertions.
- Redact or exclude credentials, direct identifiers, model/user prose, retrieved private data,
  PendingAction arguments, and server-only rejection reasons from metric labels and trace payloads.
- Add focused configuration, metric-definition, cardinality, redaction, no-op, outage, and regression
  evidence. Do not introduce a metrics dashboard or operational control plane.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every new metric has a stable name, unit, type, bounded label set, and one documented verified
   producer; unknown/future outcome values converge to a bounded class rather than a new label.
2. Identity, catalog, ordering/seckill, payment/refund, RAG, chat, and PendingAction
   prepare/clarification/decline/expiry emit the defined observations without changing their existing
   result, transaction, authorization, retry, or evidence behavior.
3. FAQ cache hits/misses and model-call savings have executable numerator/denominator definitions;
   replay, retry, denied, unavailable, and bypass cases cannot be double-counted or misclassified.
4. Trace configuration is optional and defaults safely to no-op. Missing credentials, exporter
   outage, timeout, malformed response, or shutdown cannot fail or delay the observed business path
   beyond its fixed local bound.
5. Metrics and traces contain no high-cardinality identity/business keys, credentials, private text,
   raw prompts/responses, PendingAction arguments, or server-only fault attribution.
6. No assertion, evaluation result, UI truth, retry decision, or state transition reads from metrics
   or the trace sink. Durable owner truth remains the only acceptance source.
7. No Agent confirmation/receipt/turn-commit, MemoryPacker/PII, handoff, candidate export, dashboard,
   alerting, deployment, or later measurement behavior is added.

## Rejection paths

- Reject dynamic identifiers or free text as labels, secrets/private content in exports, unbounded
  metric names, and label values derived from raw dependency/model errors.
- Reject tracing as a required dependency, trace success as evidence, retry-on-export amplification,
  and export failure changing business results.
- Reject counters for unimplemented confirmation, Agent receipt Turn commit, memory summary, handoff,
  or failure-candidate export.
- Reject ambiguous cache/model-saving formulas or measurements that cannot be reconstructed from
  the documented producer events.

## Out of scope

- dashboards, alert routing, SLO policy, production observability infrastructure, real-provider
  telemetry, log aggregation, or deployment;
- Agent successful confirmation/receipt projection/turn commit, memory/PII, handoff, candidate
  export, cart/storefront expansion, demo reset/drills, or load/quality execution.

## Required evidence

- executable inventory/cardinality tests and exact producer coverage for every metric;
- FAQ cache and model-call-saving counter matrices covering hit, miss, bypass, replay, retry,
  denial, and dependency failure;
- no-op/default, enabled fake exporter, timeout, outage, malformed-export, and shutdown tests proving
  unchanged business responses and bounded overhead;
- redaction/secret/high-cardinality rejection evidence and proof that no assertion reads the mirror;
- complete-diff self-review, fresh independent review, and final repository checks.
