Business truth for the confirmations in action_confirmation_http.txt, read with SQL against
the authoritative databases rather than inferred from the HTTP responses.

refunded_amount_minor is 0 by design: commerce durably records a refund REQUEST and settles
it asynchronously, so a receipt proves the request was committed and not that money moved.
